# Privacy Policy for GoLogger Places

**Last updated: January 25, 2026**

## Overview

GoLogger Places ("the App") is a personal location and trail tracking app developed by GoLogger. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it's used.

## Data Collection

## Location Data

The App collects GPS location data to:
- Show your current position on the map
- Record trails (GPS paths) when you choose to start a recording
- Display nearby points of interest

Location data is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

## Weather Data

The App uses Apple's WeatherKit service to display weather information for your saved locations. This is handled entirely by Apple's infrastructure.

## Wikipedia Data

The App can fetch nearby Wikipedia articles to show points of interest. These requests go directly to Wikipedia's public API.

## Data Storage

All your data (venues, trails, collections, visits) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate any servers that store your personal data.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal data to any third parties.

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete the app at any time to remove all stored data.

## Third-Party Services

- Wikipedia API - Subject to https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Privacy_policy
